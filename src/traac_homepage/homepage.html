matooke_and_chicken
Ingredients
  1. chicken
  2. royco
  3. salt
  4. matooke
  5. spices ie tomatoes , onions
Proceedures
  1. peal the matooke and cut the chicken into sizable pieces
  2. boil both them simultaneously and grind th matooke when its done
  3. after fry the chicken using the spices after which put some royco
